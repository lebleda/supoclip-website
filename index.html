Everything OpusClip does. Without the leash.

A full pipeline from raw video to ready-to-post short, running on your own hardware or our hosted instance.

AI clip selection

An LLM (Gemini, GPT, Claude, or local Ollama) picks the 3–7 most clip-worthy segments from the transcript.

Virality scoring

Every clip gets hook, engagement, value, and shareability scores so you post the best ones first.

Smart vertical crop

Face detection keeps the speaker centered in the 9:16 frame. No awkward off-center subjects.

Word-synced subtitles

AssemblyAI word-level timestamps with custom fonts, caption templates, and animation styles.

Hook titles

An AI-written headline burned into the top of each clip's opening seconds to stop the scroll.

B-roll & transitions

Optional Pexels stock footage overlays and transition effects between segments.

Built-in editor

Trim, split, and merge clips, then export with platform presets for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

Real-time progress

Live pipeline updates streamed to the browser while your video processes.

From upload to posted clip in minutes.

A simple four-step pipeline. You keep full control of every output.

01

Drop your video

Upload a podcast, talk, or stream VOD. Any length, any format supported by FFmpeg.

02

Transcribe & score

AssemblyAI transcribes with word timestamps; the LLM finds and scores the best moments.

03

Render clips

Face-centered 9:16 crop, hook titles, word-synced captions, optional B-roll and transitions.

04

Edit & export

Trim, split, merge in the built-in editor, then export for TikTok, Reels, or Shorts.
